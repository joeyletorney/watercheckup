## Weekly review (after one full week, then at 30 days)

Write dates, sample counts and changes made. Keep these questions separate:

1. Are relevant visitors reaching commercial guides or finishing the quiz? If scarce, improve useful distribution and relevant search visibility before judging conversion.
2. Are those visitors clicking through to appropriate products? If scarce, inspect fit, clarity, first comparison placement, cost and installation objections.
3. Does the merchant record the genuine referrals? If GA4 shows clicks but the merchant does not, inspect tag/account/consent/reporting differences and actual destinations before judging the products.
4. Do substantial genuine merchant referrals yield orders? Inspect availability, matching model, price, trust and attribution. Avoid a rigid universal conversion threshold; small samples are inconclusive.
5. Do ordered items become qualifying/shipped sales and commission? Review programme status, timing, returns and exclusions.

Record decisions, not just graphs. Do not claim these changes improved traffic or revenue until comparable actual data supports it. No scheduled review or external posting has been created automatically.
